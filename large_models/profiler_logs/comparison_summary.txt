KEY INSIGHTS
====================================================================================================

1. SUB-LINEAR SCALING: CUDA time scales ~4.3x while parameters scale ~39x (350M → 13B)
   - This indicates excellent GPU utilization efficiency at larger scales

2. TIME PER BILLION PARAMETERS (efficiency improves with scale):
   - OPT-350M: 835.3 ms/B (baseline)
   - OPT-2.7B: 510.7 ms/B (1.64x more efficient)
   - OPT-6.7B: 304.6 ms/B (2.74x more efficient)
   - OPT-13B:  276.7 ms/B (3.02x more efficient)

3. ZO OPERATION BREAKDOWN:
   - zo_gradient_estimation dominates (~75-80% of zo_step time)
   - zo_update scales linearly with parameters (as expected)
   - Forward passes are well-optimized

4. OPTIMIZATION OPPORTUNITIES:
   - Fused perturbation kernels can reduce zo_gradient_estimation time
   - Custom Philox RNG (from our kernel work) can speed up aten::normal_
   - Batch size reduction at larger scales may leave GPU underutilized
